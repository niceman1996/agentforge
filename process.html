How we work

Five steps, none of them a meeting. The whole process is designed to run across time zones without slowing down.

  1. 1. Tell us the job

    Email us what you want automated or built: the goal, the tools you already use, your timeline and a budget range. Ten minutes of writing is enough — a rough description is a fine starting point.

  2. 2. Fixed-price spec in 48 hours

    You get back a one-page spec and a fixed quote. If you don't like either, keep the spec and walk away — no charge, no hard feelings.

  3. 3. We build

    You receive a short recorded video update once a week showing working progress — not slideware. Questions go by email; nothing needs a meeting.

  4. 4. Delivery & walkthrough

    You get the working agent or workflow, written documentation, and a recorded demo you can replay and share with your team.

  5. 5. Two weeks of support included

    Fixes and tuning are on us for two weeks after delivery. After that, the care plan keeps things healthy if you want it — cancel monthly.

Communication, concretely

  • · Email and recorded video only — no calls required to get a build done.
  • · We work UTC+8 with strong overlap with European mornings; US East clients get end-of-day recorded updates.
  • · Every promise (scope, price, date) is written in the spec — the spec is the contract.
