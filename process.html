How we work

Five steps from your brief to a working setup on the agreed host. Routine updates happen by email and recorded video; remote installation sessions are arranged when needed.

  1. 1. Tell us the job

    Email your goal, current tools, timeline and budget range. For local deployment, include the operating system and available hardware, whether the machine can stay on, and whether cloud model APIs are acceptable. Do not email passwords or API keys.

  2. 2. Scope and hardware review

    After the brief and hosting details are complete, we aim to send a one-page scope and fixed quote within two business days. It names the deployment target, model choice, recurring costs, revision allowance and acceptance checks. Keep the initial scope and quote at no charge.

  3. 3. We build

    We build against the agreed examples and send progress by email or recorded video. You enter account credentials directly in your own environment. Any temporary setup access is agreed before use.

  4. 4. Remote installation and handover

    For self-hosted projects, we arrange remote setup on the agreed computer or server and run the acceptance checks there. You receive the configuration, source where applicable, restart and backup instructions, and a recorded walkthrough. Temporary access is removed after handover unless separately agreed for maintenance.

  5. 5. Fourteen days of bug-fix support

    Project builds include 14 days of fixes for behavior that misses the agreed scope. New features, new integrations and changes to the deployment environment are quoted separately. Ongoing care is optional, from $199 per month for up to two hours of maintenance.

Communication, concretely

  • · Email and recorded video for routine updates; agreed remote sessions for installation.
  • · We work UTC+8 and arrange installation times with you before requesting access.
  • · Scope, price, delivery date, acceptance criteria and running costs are agreed in writing.
