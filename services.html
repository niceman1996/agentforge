Services & pricing

We prioritize remote deployment on your own computer or server. All prices are in USD and are starting prices for the scope below. Once your brief and hosting details are complete, we aim to send a written scope and fixed quote within two business days. Cloud options are available when they fit your requirements.

Local deployment and running costs

Installation on one supported host is included in the self-hosted project quote. Hardware purchases, hosting, model API usage, software licenses and subscriptions are paid by you through your own accounts. We list the expected services and costs before work starts.

Local hosting can still use cloud model APIs. Fully offline inference requires a hardware and task review and must be agreed in the quote. For scheduled or always-on workflows, your host needs to stay powered and connected.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom GPT or AI agent cost?

A self-hosted AI assistant starts at $1,500. A single self-hosted n8n workflow starts at $999. Optional cloud Custom GPT setup starts at $499. These are starting prices for the stated scope; after reviewing your requirements and hardware, we agree a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does a build take?

Allow 1–3 weeks for a self-hosted assistant, 5–10 business days for one workflow, or 3–5 business days for a Custom GPT. The schedule starts once the required content, supported hardware and agreed access are ready. Your written quote confirms the delivery date.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

We agree acceptance examples and the revision allowance before work begins. Project builds include 14 days of bug fixes for behavior that misses that scope. Any payment, acceptance and refund terms are agreed in writing before the project starts; additional features need a separate quote.

Who owns the work?

You receive the custom code, prompts, workflow configuration and documentation created for your project. The installation and service accounts are under your control. Third-party software and models remain subject to their own licenses and service terms.

Where does my data live?

For self-hosted projects, the installation runs on your computer or server. That does not automatically make it fully offline: cloud model APIs and connected services receive the inputs needed for the agreed workflow. We document those data flows in the scope. You enter credentials directly, and any temporary remote access is agreed with you. NDAs are welcome.

What time zone do you work in, and how do we communicate?

We work UTC+8 and use email and recorded video for routine updates. When remote installation needs a shared session, we arrange a time with you in advance. The project quote sets the communication and handover schedule.

Can everything run locally without a cloud model?

Potentially, after reviewing the task, operating system, memory and available CPU or GPU. A suitable local model must be checked against your acceptance examples. Offline operation is agreed explicitly in the quote; it is not included automatically. Custom GPT and Make options use cloud platforms.

What ongoing costs are separate from the project price?

Hardware, hosting, model API usage, software licenses and third-party subscriptions are paid through your own accounts. We list the expected services and costs in the quote. A local host must stay powered and connected for scheduled or always-on workflows. The optional $199/month care plan includes up to two hours of maintenance; it does not include those running costs.
