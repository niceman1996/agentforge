Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom GPT or AI agent cost?

Most single-purpose agents land between $500 and $2,000 — in line with what the freelance market pays for this work. You always get a fixed quote after a written spec, before any work starts, so the number is never a surprise.

How long does a build take?

Simple agents ship in 5–10 business days. Workflow automations take 1–3 weeks depending on how many systems they connect. The spec you get in step 2 includes the delivery date.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

You approve the spec first, so surprises are rare. Every build includes two weeks of fixes. If a deliverable misses the agreed spec, we fix it or refund it — the spec is the contract.

Who owns the work?

You do. Code, prompts and documentation are handed over in full. Automations run on your own accounts, so there is no lock-in: you can maintain everything yourself or hand it to anyone else.

Where does my data live?

In your accounts. We build against infrastructure you own — your OpenAI or Anthropic account, your n8n or Make subscription. Credentials never pass through us, and we're happy to sign an NDA before the spec.

What time zone do you work in, and how do we communicate?

We work UTC+8, with strong overlap with European mornings. All communication is async-first — email and recorded video — so the ocean between us never slows the project down.
